Read before choosing a route
  • Tawang barely fits. Every source says 6+ days from Guwahati, at 12–16 h of driving each way. We'd get one day there and no buffer.
  • Meghalaya taxi dispute. Local unions have blocked Assam-registered tourist vehicles. Hire a Meghalaya-registered vehicle, or get the operator's confirmation in writing.
  • Meghalaya rules since June 2025: guides are mandatory for treks in East Khasi Hills, every hotel registers each guest (carry photo ID), and self-drive rentals are banned.
  • The festivals all miss us: Shillong Cherry Blossom is 20–21 Nov and Hornbill is 1–10 Dec. Sangai is in Manipur, which is ruled out on safety.
  • Dawki's clear water isn't guaranteed in late November. Ask the camps a week before.
  • Book the return after ~17:00 so the last day isn't spent rushing to the airport.
